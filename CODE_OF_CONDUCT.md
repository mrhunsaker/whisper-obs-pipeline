# Code of Conduct

This code of conduct is adapted from the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1, which is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We welcome contributors who use assistive technology, and we treat accessibility feedback on the tools and documentation as a normal and valued contribution.

## Our standards

Behavior that helps build a good community:

- Being kind, patient and respectful toward others
- Giving and graciously accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for mistakes, apologizing, and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community, not just for ourselves
- Assuming good intent, and asking for clarification before assuming otherwise

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and any channel that represents the project) and also when an individual is officially representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the maintainer at `<your-email>`. All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and safety of the reporter will be respected. Maintainers are obligated to respect the confidentiality of anyone who reports an incident.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not align with this code, and will explain the reason when appropriate.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will follow these steps, in order of severity, when deciding the consequences of a violation:

1. **Correction.** For inappropriate language or behavior judged unprofessional or unwelcome. *Consequence:* a private, written explanation of the problem and clarity about what was wrong. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning.** For a single incident or a series of actions. *Consequence:* a warning with consequences for continued behavior, and no interaction with the people involved, including with those enforcing this code, for a specified period. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.
3. **Temporary ban.** For a serious violation, or sustained inappropriate behavior. *Consequence:* a temporary ban from any interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period.
4. **Permanent ban.** For a pattern of violations, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals. *Consequence:* a permanent ban from any public interaction with the project.
